Game rules

Claims require a true wall-to-wall enclosure using your own buildings and/or the city wall. Corner touching does not count. The Cathedral never forms part of a boundary.

If an enclosed region contains exactly one opposing building or the Cathedral, that piece is removed immediately and you claim the region. A captured opposing building may be played again; the Cathedral is permanently removed. A region containing two or more such pieces cannot be claimed. Multiple separate regions completed by a single move are assessed independently.

Neither player may claim territory on their first building move. When neither player can place another building, the player with the lower total of unplaced squares wins.
